# Registration documents and plan enrolment

Implementation and local validation: 21–22 October 2026. This is engineering delivery, not production privacy/legal approval or approval of any normal-environment plan terms.

## Working features

Public and staff-assisted membership registration share the existing Pending workflow, generic duplicate acknowledgement, category cards, safe retries and office contacts. New fields collect National ID number, income source and private residence/income evidence, with a distinct alternative-evidence input for self-employment. Evidence consent starts unticked; its wording/version and server timestamp are retained. Assisted submissions retain their existing applicant authorisation and session-derived staff provenance. Employment evidence is not represented as a guarantee of consistent future contributions.

Uploads accept **single-image PNG/JPEG only**, maximum **2 MiB and 16 megapixels each**, at most three evidence types per submission and a **9 MiB total JSON request bound**, including chunked requests. Files must match their declared format/extension, pass full image parsing and malware scanning. Path/control-character names, unsupported types, invalid encoding, corrupt/animated/oversized images are refused. Original filenames are discarded. Original bytes are retained in private PostgreSQL `bytea` rows, never public assets, URLs or logs. Private downloads use generated attachment names, `no-store`, `nosniff` and a restrictive content-security policy. The image-validation approach follows the [Pillow security guidance](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/handbook/security.html); dependency `Pillow==13.0.0` is pinned.

The malware adapter uses private ClamAV `VERSION` and `INSTREAM` requests. A missing/unreachable scanner, stale/uninitialised signatures (older than seven days), non-clean result or timeout fails closed. `DOCUMENT_SCAN_HOST`/`DOCUMENT_SCAN_PORT` are infrastructure configuration, not business settings. No scanner bypass is available in development or production. A deterministic synthetic adapter is guarded by **all** of `APP_ENV=test`, an `isave_test_*` database and `DOCUMENT_SCAN_TEST_ONLY=true`; it is only for isolated fixtures and is labelled in the form. It is not evidence of live malware-engine accuracy.

Application review has a separate Documents section. Viewing/downloading requires `applications:read` and `documents:view`; reviewing additionally requires `documents:review`; additional/replacement evidence requires `documents:replace` and `applications:assist`. These capabilities are confined to canonical Staff/Admin roles. Members, anonymous users, finance users and staff without the necessary capabilities are denied, even with inappropriate grants where role restrictions apply. National IDs and file bytes never appear in ordinary application-detail responses.

Review outcomes are Awaiting review, Accepted, Rejected and Further information requested. Missing evidence is identified separately. Review reasons, reviewer identity and timestamps are append-only. Replacement evidence preserves the original file/reviews, records authorisation and starts Awaiting review. A stale review/replacement conflicts rather than overwriting another reviewer. No public follow-up/document lookup is introduced; corrections are handled through authorised staff.

## Configured requirements and historical applications

Admin Organisation settings adds four independently published controls: require National ID number, residence evidence, income evidence, and acceptance of all current submitted evidence before membership approval. Self-employed applicants satisfy the income requirement through alternative evidence. A document-retention period may remain **unset**. Recording a period does not implement automatic deletion.

Migration preserves existing settings snapshots byte-for-byte. Reading old settings supplies disabled defaults for the new requirements; no requirement is silently published. New registrations capture their effective requirement settings/version at submission. Membership approval checks that snapshot server-side and returns the same blockers to the review screen. Existing applications are neither retrospectively subjected to new requirements nor marked verified. Reviewer acknowledgement does not accept documents, verify fees or bypass blockers. Existing fee/payment rules remain unchanged.

**Normal uploads remain unavailable until a private scanner is configured.** Production private intake also requires HTTPS and a configured retention decision; retention/privacy operating procedures, encrypted storage/backups, signature updates and deletion/hold procedures still require review. This milestone does not claim production readiness. National ID numbers are recorded, not checked against a national registry. Identity/income-field corrections and public self-service replacement are not enabled. PDFs/office documents are unsupported.

## Plan approval, publication and enrolment

Existing incomplete plan drafts and version histories remain intact. Draft saving never publishes terms. `plans:approve` and `plans:publish` are separate Admin-only permissions/actions, each requiring explicit acknowledgement and a current version. Publication preview lists missing/unsupported decisions, effective dates, historical approval/publication actors and the impact on future applications. Scheduled versions are excluded until effective; later incomplete drafts do not hide the last effective published version.

Publication supports **member-chosen weekly targets**, **USD or ZiG (`ZWG`)**, and **eight calendar months from active enrolment**. Optional target limits remain unset unless explicitly configured. Fixed-amount, fixed-week and open-ended historical proposals can remain drafts but cannot be published through this policy. Admin must explicitly configure the supported missed-target, extra-contribution, withdrawal-access and unavailable-benefits boundaries. No suggested amounts, eligibility tests, benefits or normal plan publications are seeded.

Only effective published offers appear on public/member plan screens. Activated members submit their own plan application with an unticked acknowledgement and a chosen weekly target. The server derives member/actor from the session and rejects caller-supplied selectors/actor IDs. The complete displayed offer, exact terms version, acknowledgement and timestamp are frozen; later publications do not rewrite that acceptance. Category interest, membership registration, plan application and enrolment are separate records.

`plan-applications:review` permits protected search/pagination, reasons and plan decisions. `enrolments:activate` is a distinct activation permission/action. Account/membership activation must already be confirmed. Row/advisory locks and database uniqueness protect duplicate submissions, retries and concurrent activation; a repeat cannot restart the programme or create another history/financial entry. End dates add eight calendar months in Africa/Harare time, clamping to the final valid day of the resulting month (including leap years), rather than assuming a fixed number of weeks.

Member Overview and member plan/application screens show recorded status, exact weekly target/currency and activation/completion dates. Accepted history is preserved. Fees and savings remain separate. Neither plan approval nor activation creates a contribution, subscription, payment or balance. Weekly targets are not bills or automatic collections. Contribution posting/reconciliation is not implemented here.

Withdrawal wording states: requests become eligible only after programme completion, require at least two weeks’ notice **from the request**, and require authorised review. **Withdrawal processing remains unavailable.** There are no penalties, deductions, debt, automatic suspension, returns or benefits. Reapplication after rejection, plan switching/amendment and withdrawal processing require separate policy/workflow decisions.
